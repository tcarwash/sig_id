                  How provisional boxes work
                  

                  Tone-count and spacing hypotheses are heuristic evidence only.
                  Regular tone sets can prefill M-FSK analysis, but verify them
                  before decoding; they do not identify a protocol or guarantee
                  a decoded message. On a saved capture, use Redraw bounds and
                  redraw the corrected rectangle on the waterfall, including
                  bounds beyond the detector box; Remove box clears
                  a heuristic candidate, while Reject box records CV review feedback
                  only. Rejection does not create a negative example. CV export uses
                  accepted saved signal boxes on fully reviewed WAV windows; a
                  fully reviewed window with no saved boxes is a negative example.
